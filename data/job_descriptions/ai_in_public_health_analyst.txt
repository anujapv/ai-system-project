AI in Public Health Analyst

Job Summary
Uses artificial intelligence and machine learning to analyze health data, predict disease trends, and improve public health interventions.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop AI/ML models for disease prediction and risk assessment
• Analyze large-scale health datasets
• Support decision-making with predictive analytics
• Collaborate with data scientists and public health experts
• Evaluate AI model performance and accuracy

Qualifications
• BSc /  MSc in Data Science / AI /  Public Health
• MPH with data analytics or AI specialization preferred

Skills
• Machine learning (Python, TensorFlow)
• Data modeling and analytics
• Statistical analysis
• Problem-solving

Work Settings
• Health tech companies
• Research labs
• Government digital health initiatives
