Biostatistician

Job Summary
A Biostatistician applies statistical techniques to analyze biological and health data for research and decision-making.

Key Responsibilities
• Design statistical models for health studies
• Analyze clinical and public health data
• Interpret results and generate insights
• Support clinical trials and epidemiological research
• Ensure data accuracy and validity

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Statistics / Biostatistics / Mathematics
• MPH or PhD in Biostatistics preferred

Skills
• Advanced statistical analysis
• Programming (R, SAS, Python)
• Data visualization
• Critical thinking

Work Settings
• Pharma companies
• Research organizations
• Government health agencies
