Block Health Officer

Job Summary
Manages healthcare services and public health programs at the block (sub-district) level.

Key Responsibilities
• Supervise PHCs and sub-centers
• Implement national health programs
• Monitor maternal and child health services
• Coordinate field health workers (ASHA, ANM)
• Maintain health records and reporting

Qualifications
• MBBS / BAMS / BHMS / BSc Nursing
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Team management
• Rural healthcare knowledge
• Program implementation

Work Settings
• Block health offices
• Rural health centers
