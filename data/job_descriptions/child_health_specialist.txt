Child Health Specialist

Job Summary
Works to improve the health, nutrition, and development of infants and children.

Key Responsibilities
• Implement child health and immunization programs
• Monitor child growth and development
• Prevent and manage childhood diseases
• Promote breastfeeding and nutrition practices
• Conduct school and community health initiatives

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Public Health
• MPH or specialization in child health

Skills
• Pediatric health knowledge
• Program management
• Data monitoring
• Communication

Work Settings
• Pediatric hospitals
• Government health programs
• NGOs
