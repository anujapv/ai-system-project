Clinical Research Associate (Public Health Focus)

Job Summary
A Clinical Research Associate (CRA) manages and monitors clinical trials, ensuring compliance with protocols and regulatory standards.

Key Responsibilities
• Monitor clinical trial sites and processes
• Ensure adherence to study protocols and ethics
• Collect and verify clinical data
• Coordinate with investigators and sponsors
• Prepare trial documentation and reports

Qualifications
• BSc Life Sciences / Pharmacy / Nursing
• Certification in clinical research (e.g., GCP)

Skills
• Clinical trial management
• Regulatory knowledge
• Attention to detail
• Communication

Work Settings
• Pharmaceutical companies
• Contract Research Organizations (CROs)
• Hospitals and research centers
