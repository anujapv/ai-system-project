Community Health Officer (CHO)

Job Summary
Frontline healthcare provider delivering primary care services at Health & Wellness Centers (HWCs).

Key Responsibilities
• Provide basic clinical services and first aid
• Conduct health screenings and risk assessments
• Promote preventive healthcare practices
• Support national health programs
• Maintain patient records and reports

Qualifications
• BSc Nursing / GNM with CHO certification (Bridge Program)

Skills
• Clinical and primary care skills
• Community engagement
• Communication

Work Settings
• Health & Wellness Centers (HWCs)
• Primary Health Centers
