Community Outreach Officer

Job Summary
Acts as a bridge between healthcare organizations and the community to ensure effective delivery of health services and programs.

Key Responsibilities
• Build relationships with community groups and leaders
• Conduct door-to-door awareness and outreach activities
• Identify community health needs and gaps
• Mobilize communities for health programs (vaccination, screening)
• Collect feedback and report community responses

Qualifications
• BSc / Diploma in Public Health / Social Work / Life Sciences

Skills
• Interpersonal and communication skills
• Community engagement
• Fieldwork and coordination
• Problem-solving

Work Settings
• NGOs
• Government health programs
• Rural and urban communities
