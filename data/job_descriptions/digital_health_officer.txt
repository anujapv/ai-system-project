Digital Health Officer

Job Summary
A Digital Health Officer manages and implements digital technologies to improve healthcare delivery, data management, and patient outcomes.

Key Responsibilities
• Implement digital health systems (EHR, mobile health apps)
• Manage health information systems and data platforms
• Support telemedicine and digital health initiatives
• Ensure data privacy and security compliance
• Train healthcare staff on digital tools

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Public Health / Health Informatics / IT
• MPH with Digital Health specialization preferred

Skills
• Health IT systems (EHR, HIS)
• Data management
• Cybersecurity basics
• Project management

Work Settings
• Hospitals
• Health tech companies
• Government digital health programs
