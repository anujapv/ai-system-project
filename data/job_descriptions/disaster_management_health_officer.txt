Disaster Management Health Officer

Job Summary
Plans and manages healthcare services during disasters, ensuring preparedness, response, and recovery.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop disaster preparedness plans
• Coordinate healthcare services during disasters
• Conduct risk assessments and simulations
• Train teams in disaster response
• Ensure rehabilitation and recovery services

Qualifications
• MBBS / MPH / Disaster Management
• Specialized training in disaster preparedness

Skills
• Disaster risk management
• Strategic planning
• Coordination and leadership
• Communication

Work Settings
• Government disaster management authorities
• NGOs
• International organizations
