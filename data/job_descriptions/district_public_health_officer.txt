District Public Health Officer

Job Summary
Oversees public health activities at the district level, ensuring effective implementation of national and state health programs.

Key Responsibilities
• Supervise all public health programs in the district
• Monitor disease outbreaks and emergency response
• Manage district-level healthcare staff
• Analyze health data and prepare reports
• Ensure proper functioning of PHCs and CHCs

Qualifications
• MBBS with MPH / MD Community Medicine preferred

Skills
• Administrative leadership
• Strategic planning
• Crisis management

Work Settings
• District Health Office
• Government hospitals
