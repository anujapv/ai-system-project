Emergency Response Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for rapid response to public health emergencies such as disease outbreaks,natural disasters, and humanitarian crises.

Key Responsibilities
• Coordinate emergency health response activities
• Assess health needs during emergencies
• Deploy resources and medical support
• Work with disaster management teams
• Prepare emergency response plans

Qualifications
• MBBS / MPH / Emergency & Disaster Management
• Training in emergency response preferred

Skills
• Crisis management
• Rapid decision-making
• Leadership under pressure
• Coordination

Work Settings
• Disaster management agencies
• NGOs and UN organizations
• Emergency response teams
