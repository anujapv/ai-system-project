Environmental Health Officer (EHO)

Job Summary
An Environmental Health Officer ensures that environmental factors such as air, water,sanitation, and waste management do not negatively impact public health.

Key Responsibilities
• Inspect environmental health conditions (water, air, housing)
• Enforce public health and environmental regulations
• Investigate environmental hazards and complaints
• Monitor pollution levels and sanitation systems
• Conduct risk assessments and recommend corrective actions

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Environmental Health / Public Health / Life Sciences
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Environmental risk assessment
• Regulatory compliance
• Inspection and auditing
• Problem-solving

Work Settings
• Municipal corporations
• Pollution control boards
• Public health departments
