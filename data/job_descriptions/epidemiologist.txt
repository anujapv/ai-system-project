Epidemiologist

Job Summary
An Epidemiologist studies the distribution, patterns, and causes of diseases in populations to develop strategies for prevention and control.

Key Responsibilities
• Design and conduct epidemiological studies
• Analyze health data to identify disease trends
• Investigate causes and risk factors of diseases
• Develop prevention and control strategies
• Publish research and support policy decisions

Qualifications
• MBBS / Life Sciences / Statistics
• Master of Public Health (MPH) or MSc Epidemiology

Skills
• Biostatistics and data analysis
• Research methodology
• Critical thinking
• Data visualization tools (R, SPSS, Python)

Work Settings
• Government health departments
• Research institutions
• International health organizations
