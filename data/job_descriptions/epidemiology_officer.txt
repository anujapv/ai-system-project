Epidemiology Officer

Job Summary
Specializes in studying disease patterns, causes, and prevention strategies.

Key Responsibilities
• Investigate disease outbreaks
• Collect and analyze epidemiological data
• Design prevention strategies
• Prepare research reports
• Support public health decision-making

Qualifications
• MBBS / Life Sciences + MPH (Epidemiology specialization)

Skills
• Statistical analysis
• Research methodology
• Critical thinking

Work Settings
• Research institutes
• Government health departments
• International organizations
