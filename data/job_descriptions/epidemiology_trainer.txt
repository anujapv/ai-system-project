Epidemiology Trainer

Job Summary
Specializes in training professionals in epidemiological methods, disease surveillance, and outbreak investigation.

Key Responsibilities
• Teach epidemiology concepts and tools
• Train staff in outbreak investigation techniques
• Conduct workshops on surveillance systems
• Develop training materials and case studies
• Evaluate trainee performance

Qualifications
• MPH / MSc Epidemiology
• Experience in epidemiological research or fieldwork

Skills
• Epidemiological expertise
• Teaching and facilitation
• Data analysis
• Communication

Work Settings
• Public health institutes
• Government training programs
• International health organizations
