Family Welfare Officer

Job Summary
Manages programs related to family planning, reproductive health, and population control.

Key Responsibilities
• Promote family planning methods and services
• Implement reproductive health programs
• Conduct awareness campaigns on contraception
• Monitor population health indicators
• Coordinate sterilization and spacing programs

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Public Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Counseling and communication
• Cultural sensitivity
• Program coordination

Work Settings
• Government health departments
• Community health centers
• NGOs
