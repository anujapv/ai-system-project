Field Epidemiologist

Job Summary
A Field Epidemiologist works on-site to investigate disease outbreaks and implement control measures in real-time.

Key Responsibilities
• Conduct outbreak investigations in affected areas
• Collect field data and samples
• Identify sources and transmission patterns
• Recommend immediate control measures
• Train local health workers on surveillance

Qualifications
• MBBS / MPH (Field Epidemiology preferred)
• Training programs like Field Epidemiology Training Program (FETP)

Skills
• Field investigation skills
• Rapid decision-making
• Communication and reporting
• Crisis response

Work Settings
• Rural and urban outbreak zones
• Government and WHO programs
