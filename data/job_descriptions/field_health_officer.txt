Field Health Officer (NGO/UN Projects)

Job Summary
Operates on the ground to implement health programs in underserved or crisis-affected areas.

Key Responsibilities
• Deliver healthcare services in field settings
• Implement health interventions and campaigns
• Collect field data and report findings
• Train local healthcare workers
• Coordinate with local authorities and NGOs

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Public Health
• Experience in fieldwork preferred

Skills
• Field operations
• Adaptability and resilience
• Communication
• Problem-solving

Work Settings
• Rural and remote areas
• Disaster or conflict zones
• NGO/UN field projects
