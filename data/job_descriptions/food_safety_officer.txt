Food Safety Officer

Job Summary
Ensures that food products are safe, hygienic, and comply with regulatory standards.

Key Responsibilities
• Inspect food establishments and production units
• Collect food samples for testing
• Enforce food safety laws and regulations
• Investigate food contamination and complaints
• Educate food handlers on hygiene practices

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Food Technology / Microbiology / Public Health
• Certification aligned with Food Safety and Standards Authority of India guidelines

Skills
• Food safety regulations knowledge
• Inspection and auditing
• Analytical skills
• Attention to detail

Work Settings
• Food safety departments
• Restaurants and food industries
• Government regulatory bodies
