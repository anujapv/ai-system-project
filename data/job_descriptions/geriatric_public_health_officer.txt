Geriatric Public Health Officer

Job Summary
Focuses on improving the health and quality of life of elderly populations.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop programs for elderly healthcare and support
• Manage chronic disease prevention and care
• Promote healthy aging and lifestyle interventions
• Coordinate home-based and community care services
• Address mental and social health issues of seniors

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Public Health
• MPH or specialization in geriatric care preferred

Skills
• Geriatric care knowledge
• Program coordination
• Empathy and communication
• Healthcare management

Work Settings
• Hospitals
• Community health programs
• Elder care centers
