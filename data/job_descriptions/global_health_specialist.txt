Global Health Specialist

Job Summary
Works on international health issues, focusing on improving health outcomes across countries and regions.

Key Responsibilities
• Design and manage global health programs
• Analyze international health trends and policies
• Collaborate with global partners and agencies like World Health Organization
• Support cross-country health initiatives
• Conduct research and policy analysis

Qualifications

• Experience in global health programs

Skills
• Cross-cultural communication
• Policy analysis
• Research and data analysis
• Strategic planning

Work Settings
• International organizations (WHO, UNICEF, World Bank)
• Global NGOs
• Research institutions
