Government Health Advisor

Job Summary
Provides expert advice to government bodies on healthcare policies, programs, and public health strategies.

Key Responsibilities
• Advise policymakers on health-related issues
• Develop national and state health strategies
• Analyze healthcare challenges and solutions
• Collaborate with ministries and stakeholders
• Represent government in health forums and meetings

Qualifications
• MBBS / MPH / MD Community Medicine
• Advanced experience in public health policy

Skills
• Strategic thinking
• Policy expertise
• Communication and negotiation
• Leadership

Work Settings
• Government ministries
• Policy advisory bodies
• International health organizations
