Health Data Analyst

Job Summary
A Health Data Analyst processes and analyzes healthcare data to support decision-making and improve health outcomes.

Key Responsibilities
• Collect and clean healthcare datasets
• Analyze trends, patterns, and outcomes
• Develop dashboards and reports
• Support policy and operational decisions
• Ensure data quality and integrity

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Data Science / Public Health / Statistics
• Certifications in data analytics preferred

Skills
• Data analysis tools (Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI)
• Data visualization
• Problem-solving
• Analytical thinking

Work Settings
• Hospitals
• Health tech companies
• Government agencies
