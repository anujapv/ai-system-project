Health Education Officer

Job Summary
A Health Education Officer designs and delivers educational programs to improve public awareness about health, hygiene, and disease prevention.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop health education materials and campaigns
• Conduct workshops, seminars, and awareness sessions
• Promote preventive healthcare practices
• Collaborate with healthcare providers and community leaders
• Evaluate the impact of health education programs

Qualifications
• BSc Public Health / Health Education / Life Sciences
• Master of Public Health (MPH) preferred

Skills
• Communication and presentation
• Program planning
• Content development
• Community engagement

Work Settings
• Government health departments
• NGOs
• Schools and community centers
