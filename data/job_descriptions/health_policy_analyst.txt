Health Policy Analyst

Job Summary
A Health Policy Analyst researches, develops, and evaluates healthcare policies to improve public health systems and outcomes.

Key Responsibilities
• Analyze existing health policies and regulations
• Conduct research on healthcare systems and reforms
• Develop policy recommendations based on data
• Assess the impact of health policies
• Prepare policy briefs and reports for decision-makers

Qualifications
• BSc / BA in Public Health / Economics / Political Science
• Master of Public Health (MPH) / Health Policy / Public Policy

Skills
• Policy analysis
• Research and data interpretation
• Critical thinking
• Report writing

Work Settings
• Government ministries
• Think tanks
• International organizations
