Health Program Auditor

Job Summary
Evaluates public health programs to ensure compliance, efficiency, and effectiveness.

Key Responsibilities
• Conduct audits of health programs and services
• Review compliance with policies and standards
• Assess program performance and outcomes
• Identify gaps, risks, and inefficiencies
• Provide recommendations for improvement

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Healthcare Management / Auditing
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Audit and evaluation techniques
• Analytical thinking
• Attention to detail
• Report writing

Work Settings
• Government agencies
• NGOs
• International organizations
• Healthcare consulting firms
