Health Promotion Specialist

Job Summary
Designs and implements strategies to promote healthy lifestyles and reduce risk factors for diseases.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop health promotion strategies and campaigns
• Address lifestyle-related issues (nutrition, exercise, smoking)
• Analyze health behavior data
• Work with media and digital platforms for awareness
• Evaluate effectiveness of health promotion initiatives

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Health Promotion
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Behavioral science knowledge
• Campaign planning
• Data analysis
• Digital communication

Work Settings
• Public health organizations
• Corporate wellness programs
• NGOs and international agencies
