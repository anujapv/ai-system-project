Health Services Manager

Job Summary
Responsible for planning, directing, and coordinating healthcare services in hospitals, clinics,or health systems.

Key Responsibilities
• Manage healthcare service delivery systems
• Optimize patient care processes
• Supervise healthcare staff and departments
• Develop operational strategies
• Monitor performance and efficiency

Qualifications
• BHA / BSc Health Administration
• MBA Healthcare / MHA

Skills
• Operations management
• Leadership
• Data-driven decision-making
• Communication

Work Settings
• Hospitals
• Clinics
• Healthcare organizations
