Health Surveillance Officer

Job Summary
Monitors and tracks public health data to detect and prevent disease outbreaks.

Key Responsibilities
• Collect and analyze health surveillance data
• Monitor disease trends and alerts
• Coordinate with labs and hospitals
• Prepare surveillance reports
• Support outbreak response teams

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Life Sciences
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Data management
• Analytical skills
• Attention to detail

Work Settings
• Disease surveillance units
• Public health departments
