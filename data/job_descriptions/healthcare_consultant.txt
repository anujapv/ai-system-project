Healthcare Consultant (Public Health)

Job Summary
Provides expert guidance to organizations on improving healthcare systems, public health programs, and policies.

Key Responsibilities
• Analyze healthcare systems and identify gaps
• Recommend strategic improvements
• Support implementation of public health initiatives
• Conduct program evaluations and audits
• Prepare reports and presentations for stakeholders

Qualifications
• MPH / MBA Healthcare / MD Community Medicine
• Relevant experience in consulting or healthcare management

Skills
• Strategic thinking
• Problem-solving
• Data analysis
• Communication and presentation

Work Settings
• Consulting firms
• NGOs
• Government agencies
• International organizations
