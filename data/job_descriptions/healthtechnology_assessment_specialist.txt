Health Technology Assessment Specialist

Job Summary
Evaluates medical technologies, devices, and interventions for effectiveness, cost-efficiency,and impact on public health.

Key Responsibilities
• Assess new healthcare technologies and interventions
• Conduct cost-effectiveness and impact analysis
• Review clinical and economic evidence
• Support policy decisions on technology adoption
• Prepare assessment reports for stakeholders

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Health Economics / Life Sciences
• MPH or specialization in health economics preferred

Skills
• Health economics
• Evidence-based analysis
• Research and evaluation
• Report writing

Work Settings
• Government agencies
• Health policy organizations
• Pharmaceutical and medical device companies
