HIV/AIDS Program Officer

Job Summary
Manages programs focused on prevention, treatment, and awareness of HIV/AIDS.

Key Responsibilities
• Implement HIV/AIDS prevention and control programs
• Promote testing, counseling, and ART services
• Monitor program performance and patient outcomes
• Coordinate with NGOs and community organizations
• Conduct awareness campaigns to reduce stigma

Qualifications
• MBBS / Life Sciences / Public Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Program coordination
• Counseling and communication
• Data analysis

Work Settings
• Government programs (e.g., NACO)
• NGOs and international organizations
• ART centers and hospitals
