IEC (Information, Education,Communication) Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for developing and managing IEC strategies to effectively communicate health messages to the public.

Key Responsibilities
• Design IEC materials (posters, brochures, videos)
• Plan communication strategies for health campaigns
• Coordinate with media and communication teams
• Ensure consistent messaging across platforms
• Monitor and evaluate communication impact

Qualifications
• BSc / BA in Public Health / Mass Communication / Social Sciences
• MPH or specialization in health communication preferred

Skills
• Content creation and storytelling
• Media and communication strategy
• Graphic and digital tools knowledge
• Creativity

Work Settings
• Government health departments
• NGOs
• Media and communication agencies
