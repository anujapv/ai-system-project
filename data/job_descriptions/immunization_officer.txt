Immunization Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for planning and managing vaccination programs to prevent infectious diseases.

Key Responsibilities
• Plan and implement immunization campaigns
• Ensure vaccine supply and cold chain management
• Monitor vaccination coverage and effectiveness
• Train healthcare workers on immunization protocols
• Conduct awareness programs

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Public Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Vaccine logistics management
• Program coordination
• Data tracking and reporting
• Community engagement

Work Settings
• Government immunization programs
• WHO/UNICEF projects
• Hospitals and PHCs
