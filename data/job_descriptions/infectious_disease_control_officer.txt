Infectious Disease Control Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for preventing and controlling infectious diseases through surveillance,intervention programs, and public health policies.

Key Responsibilities
• Monitor infectious disease trends
• Develop and implement control strategies
• Coordinate infection prevention programs
• Educate the public and healthcare workers
• Ensure compliance with infection control guidelines

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Life Sciences
• MPH or specialization in infectious diseases

Skills
• Infection control practices
• Program management
• Risk assessment
• Communication

Work Settings
• Hospitals
• Public health departments
• NGOs
