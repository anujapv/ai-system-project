39. Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) Officer

Job Summary
An M&E Officer assesses the performance and impact of public health programs using data-driven approaches.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop monitoring frameworks and indicators
• Track program progress and outcomes
• Conduct evaluations and impact assessments
• Prepare reports for stakeholders and donors
• Recommend improvements based on findings

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Statistics / Economics
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Data analysis and reporting
• Program evaluation techniques
• Logical framework (LogFrame) approach
• Critical thinking

Work Settings
• NGOs
• Government programs
• International organizations
