18. Maternal & Child Health (MCH) Officer

Job Summary
Focuses on improving the health of mothers, infants, and children through targeted public health programs.

Key Responsibilities
• Implement maternal and child health programs
• Monitor antenatal, postnatal, and child healthcare services
• Reduce maternal and infant mortality rates
• Promote institutional deliveries and immunization
• Train healthcare workers (ANMs, ASHAs)

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Public Health
• MPH with MCH specialization preferred

Skills
• Program implementation
• Community health knowledge
• Monitoring and evaluation

Work Settings
• Government hospitals
• Public health departments
• NGOs
