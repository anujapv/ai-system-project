Maternal Health Specialist

Job Summary
Focuses on improving the health and well-being of pregnant women and mothers through targeted healthcare programs and interventions.

Key Responsibilities
• Design and implement maternal health programs
• Monitor antenatal and postnatal care services
• Reduce maternal mortality and complications
• Promote institutional deliveries and safe childbirth practices
• Train healthcare workers (ANMs, nurses, midwives)

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Public Health
• MPH with Maternal Health specialization preferred

Skills
• Maternal healthcare knowledge
• Program implementation
• Community engagement
• Monitoring & evaluation

Work Settings
• Government hospitals
• Public health programs
• NGOs
