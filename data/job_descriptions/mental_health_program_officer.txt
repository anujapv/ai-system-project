Mental Health Program Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for planning and implementing mental health programs and promoting psychological well-being.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop mental health awareness programs
• Coordinate counseling and psychiatric services
• Reduce stigma associated with mental illness
• Monitor mental health indicators and outcomes
• Train healthcare workers in mental health care

Qualifications
• Psychology / Psychiatry / Public Health
• MPH or MSc Psychology preferred

Skills
• Mental health knowledge
• Counseling and communication
• Program management
• Empathy

Work Settings
• Mental health institutions
• NGOs
• Government health programs
