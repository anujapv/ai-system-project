Municipal Health Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for maintaining public health standards within a municipality or urban local body.

Key Responsibilities
• Oversee sanitation, waste management, and hygiene
• Monitor food safety and water quality
• Conduct urban disease surveillance
• Manage public health staff in municipalities
• Enforce public health laws

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Public Health / Environmental Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Environmental health expertise
• Regulatory knowledge
• Field inspection skills

Work Settings
• Municipal corporations
• Urban local bodies
