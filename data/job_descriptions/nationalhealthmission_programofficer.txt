National Health Mission (NHM) Program Officer
Job Summary
Responsible for implementing and monitoring programs under India’s National Health Mission to improve healthcare delivery.

Key Responsibilities
• Plan and execute NHM initiatives (RMNCH+A, disease control, etc.)
• Monitor program indicators and outcomes
• Coordinate with state and district health teams
• Manage funding and reporting under NHM guidelines
• Support training and capacity building

Qualifications
• MBBS / BDS / BSc Nursing / Life Sciences
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Government program management
• Data analysis and reporting
• Stakeholder coordination

Work Settings
• State & District Health Societies
• Government health offices
