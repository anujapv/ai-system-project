NGO Founder (Health Sector)

Job Summary
Establishes and leads a non-governmental organization focused on improving public health outcomes.

Key Responsibilities
• Define mission, vision, and goals of the NGO
• Design and implement health programs
• Secure funding through grants and donors
• Build partnerships with government and organizations
• Manage operations, staff, and finances

Qualifications
• Public Health / Social Work / Healthcare background
• MPH or leadership experience preferred

Skills
• Leadership and entrepreneurship
• Fundraising and networking
• Strategic planning
• Program management

Work Settings
• Self-run organization
• Community-based or national initiatives
