Nutrition & Diet Public Health Officer

Job Summary
Works on improving nutritional status and preventing diet-related diseases at the community level.

Key Responsibilities
• Design nutrition intervention programs
• Address malnutrition and micronutrient deficiencies
• Promote balanced diets and healthy eating habits
• Monitor nutrition indicators (BMI, anemia, etc.)
• Coordinate with government nutrition schemes

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Nutrition / Dietetics / Public Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Nutrition science knowledge
• Community outreach
• Data analysis
• Program implementation

Work Settings
• Government nutrition programs
• NGOs
• Community health initiatives
