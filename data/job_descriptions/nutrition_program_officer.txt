Nutrition Program Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for planning and implementing nutrition-related public health programs to combat malnutrition.

Key Responsibilities
• Design nutrition intervention programs
• Monitor malnutrition and dietary trends
• Coordinate with schemes like ICDS
• Conduct awareness on balanced diets
• Evaluate program effectiveness

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Nutrition / Dietetics / Public Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Nutrition science knowledge
• Data monitoring and reporting
• Community outreach

Work Settings
• Government nutrition programs
• NGOs (UNICEF, WHO projects)
• Community health initiatives
