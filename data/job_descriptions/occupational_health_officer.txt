Occupational Health Officer

Job Summary
Ensures the health and safety of employees in workplaces by preventing occupational hazards and promoting wellness.

Key Responsibilities
• Conduct workplace health risk assessments
• Monitor employee health and safety standards
• Implement occupational health programs
• Investigate workplace accidents and hazards
• Ensure compliance with labor and safety regulations

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Nursing / Occupational Health
• Diploma/MD in Occupational Health (preferred)

Skills
• Workplace safety management
• Risk assessment
• Emergency response planning
• Communication

Work Settings
• Industries and factories
• Corporate organizations
• Construction and manufacturing units
