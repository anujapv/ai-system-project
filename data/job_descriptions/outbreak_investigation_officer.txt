Outbreak Investigation Officer

Job Summary
Specializes in identifying, investigating, and controlling disease outbreaks in communities.

Key Responsibilities
• Conduct rapid outbreak investigations
• Collect epidemiological and clinical data
• Trace contacts and identify infection sources
• Recommend containment strategies
• Prepare detailed investigation reports

Qualifications
• MBBS / MPH (Epidemiology)
Skills
• Analytical and investigative skills
• Fieldwork and sampling
• Crisis management
• Communication

Work Settings
• Emergency response teams
• Public health departments
• Disaster management units
