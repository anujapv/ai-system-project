Program Coordinator (NGO)

Job Summary
Coordinates and manages health programs within NGOs, ensuring smooth implementation and impact at the community level.

Key Responsibilities
• Plan and execute NGO health projects
• Coordinate with field teams and stakeholders
• Monitor project progress and timelines
• Manage budgets and reporting
• Organize training and awareness activities

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Social Work / Life Sciences
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Project coordination
• Team management
• Communication
• Organizational skills

Work Settings
• Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)
• Community-based organizations
