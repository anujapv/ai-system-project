Program Evaluation Officer

Job Summary
Evaluates public health programs to determine their effectiveness, efficiency, and impact.

Key Responsibilities
• Design evaluation frameworks and methodologies
• Assess program outcomes and performance
• Conduct impact and cost-effectiveness analysis
• Prepare evaluation reports
• Recommend improvements for programs

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Statistics / Economics
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Evaluation techniques
• Data analysis
• Report writing
• Critical thinking

Work Settings
• Government agencies
• NGOs
• International organizations
