Public Health Administrator

Job Summary
Manages healthcare facilities and public health programs, ensuring efficient operations and service delivery.

Key Responsibilities
• Oversee daily operations of health departments or facilities
• Manage staff, budgets, and resources
• Implement health policies and programs
• Ensure compliance with regulations and standards
• Improve healthcare service quality

Qualifications
• BSc / BHA (Bachelor of Hospital Administration)
• MPH / MHA (Master of Hospital Administration)

Skills
• Leadership and management
• Financial planning
• Organizational skills
• Problem-solving

Work Settings
• Hospitals
• Public health departments
• Healthcare organizations
