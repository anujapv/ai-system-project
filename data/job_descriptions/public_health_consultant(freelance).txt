Public Health Consultant (Freelance)

Job Summary
An independent expert who advises organizations on public health programs, policies, and strategies on a project basis.

Key Responsibilities
• Analyze public health challenges and propose solutions
• Design and evaluate health programs
• Support grant writing and project proposals
• Provide training and advisory services
• Conduct research and impact assessments

Qualifications
• Master of Public Health (MPH) / MD Community Medicine
• Relevant experience in public health projects

Skills
• Strategic thinking
• Problem-solving
• Data analysis
• Client communication

Work Settings
• Freelance / Remote projects
• NGOs, startups, government contracts
• Consulting assignments
