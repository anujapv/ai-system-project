Public Health Consultant

Job Summary
A Public Health Consultant provides expert advice to organizations on designing,implementing, and improving health programs and policies.

Key Responsibilities
• Assess public health programs and identify gaps
• Provide strategic recommendations and solutions
• Design project proposals and implementation plans
• Support monitoring, evaluation, and reporting
• Collaborate with governments, NGOs, and donors

Qualifications
• Master of Public Health (MPH) / MD Community Medicine
• Relevant experience in public health projects

Skills
• Strategic planning
• Problem-solving
• Data analysis
• Communication and presentation

Work Settings
• NGOs
• Consulting firms
• International organizations
