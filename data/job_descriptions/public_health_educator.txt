Public Health Educator

Job Summary
A Public Health Educator focuses on educating individuals and communities about health issues and promoting healthy lifestyles.

Key Responsibilities
• Teach health education in schools or communities
• Develop educational content on diseases and prevention
• Conduct outreach programs and campaigns
• Advocate for healthy behavior changes
• Assess community health education needs

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Public Health / Health Education
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Teaching and facilitation
• Behavioral change communication
• Creativity and content design
• Cultural awareness

Work Settings
• Schools and colleges
• NGOs
• Public health organizations
