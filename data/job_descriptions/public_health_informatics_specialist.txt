Public Health Informatics Specialist

Job Summary
Integrates public health, information technology, and data analytics to improve health system efficiency and decision-making.

Key Responsibilities
• Design and manage health information systems
• Analyze large public health datasets
• Improve data collection and reporting systems
• Support surveillance and decision-making processes
• Ensure interoperability between systems

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Health Informatics / Public Health / Data Science
• MPH or specialization in informatics preferred

Skills
• Database management (SQL)
• Data analytics (R, Python)
• Health information systems
• Systems integration

Work Settings
• Government health departments
• Research organizations
• Health IT companies
