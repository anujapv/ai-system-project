Public Health Lecturer / Professor

Job Summary
A Public Health Lecturer/Professor teaches academic courses, conducts research, and mentors students in public health disciplines.

Key Responsibilities
• Deliver lectures on public health subjects (epidemiology, biostatistics, etc.)
• Develop curriculum and academic content
• Conduct and publish research studies
• Guide student projects and dissertations
• Participate in academic committees and conferences

Qualifications
• Master of Public Health (MPH) / MSc Public Health (minimum for Lecturer)
• PhD in Public Health (preferred for Professor roles)

Skills
• Teaching and presentation
• Research and academic writing
• Mentorship
• Subject expertise

Work Settings
• Universities and colleges
• Public health institutes
• Research institutions
