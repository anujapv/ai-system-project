Public Health Officer (PHO)

Job Summary
A Public Health Officer is responsible for planning, implementing, and monitoring public health programs to improve community health and prevent diseases.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop and implement public health policies and programs
• Monitor disease outbreaks and health trends
• Conduct health inspections and risk assessments
• Coordinate vaccination and awareness campaigns
• Collaborate with hospitals, NGOs, and government bodies
• Ensure compliance with health regulations

Qualifications
• MBBS / BDS / BSc Nursing / Life Sciences
• Master of Public Health (MPH) preferred

Skills
• Epidemiology knowledge
• Leadership and communication
• Data analysis
• Problem-solving

Work Settings
• Government health departments
• Public health agencies
• NGOs
