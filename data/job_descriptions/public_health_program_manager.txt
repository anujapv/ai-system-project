Public Health Program Manager

Job Summary
Leads the planning, implementation, and evaluation of public health programs at regional,national, or organizational levels.

Key Responsibilities
• Design and execute public health programs
• Manage budgets, resources, and timelines
• Monitor program performance using KPIs
• Coordinate with stakeholders (government, NGOs, donors)
• Ensure compliance with health policies and standards
• Prepare reports and program evaluations

Qualifications
• MBBS / BSc Health Sciences / Life Sciences
• Master of Public Health (MPH) / MBA Healthcare

Skills
• Project management
• Leadership and team coordination
• Data-driven decision-making
• Communication and reporting

Work Settings
• Government health departments
• NGOs / International organizations
• Healthcare consulting firms
