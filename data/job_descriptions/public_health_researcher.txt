Public Health Researcher

Job Summary
A Public Health Researcher conducts studies to understand health trends, disease patterns,and the effectiveness of healthcare interventions.

Key Responsibilities
• Design and conduct research studies (quantitative & qualitative)
• Collect, analyze, and interpret public health data
• Publish research papers and reports
• Evaluate health programs and interventions
• Collaborate with academic and government institutions

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Public Health / Life Sciences
• Master of Public Health (MPH) / PhD preferred

Skills
• Research methodology
• Biostatistics
• Scientific writing
• Data analysis tools (R, SPSS, Python)

Work Settings
• Research institutes
• Universities
• Government organizations
• International agencies
