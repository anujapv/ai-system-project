Public Health Trainer

Job Summary
A Public Health Trainer provides practical training to healthcare professionals, field workers,and students on public health practices and programs.

Key Responsibilities
• Conduct training sessions and workshops
• Develop training materials and modules
• Train field workers (ASHA, ANM, healthcare staff)
• Evaluate training effectiveness
• Update training content based on new guidelines

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Nursing / Life Sciences
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Training and facilitation
• Communication
• Curriculum development
• Field knowledge

Work Settings
• Government training institutes
• NGOs
• Healthcare organizations
