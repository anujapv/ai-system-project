Public Health Content Creator / Educator

Job Summary
Creates educational content on public health topics through digital platforms to raise awareness and educate the public.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop content (videos, blogs, podcasts, courses)
• Simplify complex health topics for public understanding
• Build and engage an online audience
• Collaborate with healthcare professionals and brands
• Stay updated with health trends and research

Qualifications
• Public Health / Life Sciences / Communication
• MPH preferred but not mandatory

Skills
• Content creation and storytelling
• Digital marketing
• Communication and teaching
• Social media management

Work Settings
• YouTube, Instagram, blogs, online platforms
• Freelance or independent creator
• EdTech or health media platforms
