Sanitary Inspector / Sanitation Officer

Job Summary
Responsible for maintaining sanitation and hygiene standards in communities, public places, and institutions.

Key Responsibilities
• Inspect public areas for cleanliness and sanitation
• Monitor waste disposal systems
• Enforce sanitation laws and regulations
• Supervise sanitation workers and operations
• Conduct awareness campaigns on hygiene practices

Qualifications
• Diploma / BSc in Sanitary Inspection / Public Health
• Certification in sanitation or hygiene

Skills
• Field inspection
• Team supervision
• Communication and enforcement
• Attention to detail

Work Settings
• Municipal bodies
• Urban local authorities
• Hospitals and institutions
