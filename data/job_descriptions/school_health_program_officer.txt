School Health Program Officer

Job Summary
Ensures the health and well-being of school children through preventive healthcare and health education programs.

Key Responsibilities
• Conduct school health screenings (vision, dental, general health)
• Implement vaccination and nutrition programs
• Promote hygiene and healthy habits among students
• Train teachers and staff on health issues
• Monitor health records and reports

Qualifications
• BSc Nursing / Public Health / Life Sciences
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Child health knowledge
• Communication and teaching
• Program coordination
• Preventive healthcare

Work Settings
• Schools
• Government school health programs
• NGOs
