State Public Health Officer

Job Summary
Leads and manages public health initiatives at the state level.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop state-wide health policies
• Supervise district health departments
• Allocate resources and budgets
• Monitor large-scale health programs
• Coordinate with central government agencies

Qualifications
• MBBS + MPH / MD Community Medicine

Skills
• Policy-making
• Leadership
• Strategic planning

Work Settings
• State health departments
• Government ministries
