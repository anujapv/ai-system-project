Surveillance Data Analyst

Job Summary
Focuses on analyzing disease surveillance data to detect trends, outbreaks, and public health threats.

Key Responsibilities
• Analyze surveillance system data (IDSP, etc.)
• Identify abnormal trends and early warning signals
• Generate reports and alerts for authorities
• Collaborate with epidemiologists
• Maintain surveillance databases

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Statistics / Data Science
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Data monitoring and analysis
• Epidemiological knowledge
• Database management
• Attention to detail

Work Settings
• Government surveillance units
• Public health organizations
