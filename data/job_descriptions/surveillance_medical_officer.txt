Surveillance Medical Officer

Job Summary
A Surveillance Medical Officer monitors disease surveillance systems and ensures accurate data collection and reporting.

Key Responsibilities
• Oversee disease surveillance programs (e.g., IDSP)
• Collect and validate health data from multiple sources
• Identify early warning signals of outbreaks
• Coordinate with laboratories and hospitals
• Prepare surveillance reports

Qualifications
• MBBS (preferred)
• MPH is an added advantage

Skills
• Data analysis and interpretation
• Reporting and documentation
• Attention to detail
• IT and database management

Work Settings
• Government surveillance units
• National health programs
