Telehealth Program Coordinator

Job Summary
Coordinates telemedicine services and virtual healthcare programs to ensure accessible and efficient remote care.

Key Responsibilities
• Manage telehealth platforms and services
• Coordinate virtual consultations and patient scheduling
• Train healthcare providers on telemedicine tools
• Monitor telehealth performance metrics
• Ensure compliance with telehealth regulations

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Public Health / Healthcare Administration / IT
• Experience in telemedicine preferred

Skills
• Telehealth systems management
• Communication and coordination
• Technical troubleshooting
• Patient engagement

Work Settings
• Hospitals
• Telemedicine companies
• Digital health startups
