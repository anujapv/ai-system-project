Tuberculosis (TB) Control Officer

Job Summary
Leads efforts to prevent, detect, and treat tuberculosis under national TB control programs.

Key Responsibilities
• Implement TB control programs (screening, diagnosis, treatment)
• Monitor TB case detection and treatment success rates
• Ensure adherence to DOTS strategy
• Coordinate with labs and healthcare providers
• Conduct awareness campaigns

Qualifications
• MBBS preferred
• MPH / experience in TB programs

Skills
• Disease control expertise
• Program monitoring
• Data management

Work Settings
• Government TB control programs (e.g., NTEP)
• Hospitals and clinics
• NGOs
