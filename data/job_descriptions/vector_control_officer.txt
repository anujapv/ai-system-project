Vector Control Officer

Job Summary
Focuses on controlling vector-borne diseases by managing and reducing disease-carrying organisms like mosquitoes.

Key Responsibilities
• Plan vector control strategies (e.g., malaria, dengue)
• Conduct field inspections and surveillance
• Implement spraying and larval control programs
• Monitor environmental risk factors
• Educate communities on prevention methods

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Zoology, Life Sciences, Public Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Environmental health knowledge
• Fieldwork skills
• Data collection and analysis
• Public awareness communication

Work Settings
• Municipal bodies
• Public health departments
• Environmental health agencies
