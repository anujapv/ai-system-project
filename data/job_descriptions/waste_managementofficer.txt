Waste Management Officer (BiomedicalWaste)

Job Summary
Manages the safe handling, treatment, and disposal of biomedical waste to prevent environmental contamination and health risks.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop biomedical waste management plans
• Ensure segregation, storage, and disposal compliance
• Monitor waste treatment facilities
• Train healthcare staff on waste handling protocols
• Conduct audits and inspections

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc Environmental Science / Public Health
• Certification in biomedical waste management

Skills
• Waste management systems knowledge
• Regulatory compliance
• Training and supervision
• Risk management

Work Settings
• Hospitals and clinics
• Waste treatment facilities
• Environmental agencies
