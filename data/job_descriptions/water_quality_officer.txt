Water Quality Officer

Job Summary
Ensures safe drinking water by monitoring and maintaining water quality standards.

Key Responsibilities
• Test water samples for contamination
• Monitor water treatment processes
• Ensure compliance with safety standards
• Investigate waterborne disease outbreaks
• Maintain water quality reports and documentation

Qualifications
• BSc / MSc in Environmental Science / Chemistry / Public Health
• MPH preferred

Skills
• Laboratory testing and analysis
• Data interpretation
• Attention to detail
• Problem-solving

Work Settings
• Water supply departments
• Environmental labs
• Government agencies
